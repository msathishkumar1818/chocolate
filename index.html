Ancestral Method • Four Acts of Mastery

The Bean-to-Bar Alchemy

We reject industrially deodorized cocoa liquor. Every tablet produced inside our studio follows a patient, uncompromising four-stage ancestral transformation.

01 • Direct Harvest → 02 • Stone Conching → 03 • Marble Tempering → 04 • 24K Hand Finishing
Origin: Ecuador, Madagascar & Peru
01 — Act I: Sanctuaries of Origin

Direct-Trade Harvest & Cedar Fermentation

We partner directly with family-run agroforestry sanctuaries in Ecuador, Madagascar, and Peru, paying 220% above fair-trade floor prices. Wet pods are cracked open by hand within 2 hours of harvest, and beans are fermented in wild Andean cedar boxes for precisely 6 days to develop complex tropical fruit esters.

Floor Premium +220%
Fermentation 6 Days
Pod Cultivar Pure Criollo
02 — Act II: Vintage Melanging

72-Hour Granite Stone Conching

Nibs undergo gentle, slow-drum roasting to release volatile tannins without burning floral top-notes. Transferred to our 1948 vintage Italian granite conches, heavy stone wheels grind the cacao continuously for 72 hours, reducing cocoa solids down to sub-18-micron silkiness with zero soy lecithin or artificial emulsifiers.

Conching Time 72 Hours
Particle Size < 18 μm
Ingredients 100% Pure
Equipment: 1948 Antique Granite Melangeur
Surface: Cold Carrara Marble Tabling
03 — Act III: Thermodynamic Tabling

Granite Tempering & Beta-V Alignment

Tempering is chocolate physics made tactile. Our chocolatiers pour molten chocolate across cold Carrara marble slabs, spreading and agitating it with stainless palette knives. This precise thermal agitation forces the cocoa butter fatty acids into the tightly packed Beta-V crystal polymorph, guaranteeing a clean audible snap and mirror gloss.

Thermal Curve 45→27→31.5°C
Crystal Form Beta-V (Form 5)
Finish Polish Mirror Luster
04 — Act IV: Haute Confectionery

Hand-Finishing & 24K Gold Adornment

Cast into custom geometric tablet molds or hand-dipped into botanical silk ganaches. Each confection is elevated with edible 24-karat French gold leaf, hand-flaked Maldon sea salt, and candied orange peel. Every single batch is individually inspected and approved by Maître Chocolatier Henri Vance before packaging.
